# Punch list

Reviewed 2026-11-16 against `docs/STYLE.md`, build `527f350` (the living room, first playable).
Top item is the next thing built. See `docs/REVIEW.md`.

1. Crispee slides; four consecutive frames of movement show identical legs, body height and tail. Add a leg cycle, body bob and head lag. [S1]
2. No claim is performed: no walk-up, sniff, leg lift, growing puddle or shake. The slipper claim is one lean and a static yellow disc, and the thermostat claim is hidden under a screen-filling Pee Vision sphere. [S1]
3. Dad is a faceless two-box stack who teleports into place; the box move and door opening happen off-camera, so every consequence is read in the toast, not watched. [S2]
4. No title card or flyover; the level starts cold with the full HUD list, and ends on a generic Time/Best results box. [S3]
5. HUD spot names are inventory labels ("The couch cushion", "The thermostat") with no attitude, and the prompts "Act: bark at Dad", "Claim: The thermostat", "Refilled." are engine strings. [S3]
6. UI is a stock white sans with drop shadow; swap every HUD, toast and button label to one rounded hand-made face. [S6]
7. No evening light: the key is high and white, shadows short and grey. Lower it, warm it, stretch the shadows across the floor. [S6]
8. Crispee's face and silhouette are undetailed: no beard wedge, tuft, cloudy eyes, cape ties or worried expression. The AC box is a plain blue cube without its print, and there is no photo on the wall. [S6]
9. Camera goes to a straight top-down view during the thermostat claim and is jammed against Dad or the door jamb with the dog hidden. Cap the rise so furniture still looms and the dog stays in frame. [S5]
10. Buttons are drawn over the box, table and lamp, the toast overlaps JUMP, and "TERRITORY 1 · THE LIVING ROOM" and "FEEL" are under 14 px. [S7]

**Reviewer's verdict:** a stranger would see an orange room and a blocky dog in a red cape and might guess "dog game," but nothing he does on screen tells them this is CRISPEE rather than any prototype platformer with a good copywriter.

## Section notes from the review

- **S1 Dog motion.** The only body animation in the clip is a lean with a flared cape during the slipper claim. No idle behaviour during the two-second pause on the box. The tail is a fixed stick.
- **S2 World reacts.** The box move and the door opening do happen in the world, but the player never sees them happen. The thermostat toast stays up past the results card.
- **S3 Voice.** The claim toasts are the strongest thing in the build ("He thinks it is a step. It is."). Everything else reads as engine text.
- **S4 Sound.** Only the Pee Vision drone exists. By the guide's rule every step, claim, jump, bark and door swing is not done.
- **S5 Camera.** Default framing is too high; the couch and table sit small in the middle distance rather than looming.
- **S6 Look.** Orange walls, black couch, blue box, warm floor and the pee yellow are right. Light, Crispee's face, the box print and the wall photo are missing. Dad has no face or arms.
- **S7 Phone.** Buttons and stick are placed right. They sit over the action in some framings.
